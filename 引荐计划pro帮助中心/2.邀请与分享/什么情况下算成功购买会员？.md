# 什么情况下算成功购买会员？

新用户在注册之日起一年（365 天）内，通过官方会员订阅、App内购、会员码、体验会员码开通会员视为成功购买会员。但通过免费兑换码、赠送会员时间等得到免费会员资格，不被视为购买会员，也不会产生分成收益。

最终结果以系统订单状态和有效性判断为准。



---

## English

### What counts as a successful Premium purchase?

A new user is considered to have successfully purchased Premium if they activate Premium within one year (365 days) of signing up through an official Premium subscription, an in-app purchase, a Premium Code, or an Experience Premium Code. Premium obtained through a free Redeem Code, gifted Premium time, or another free method is not considered a Premium purchase and will not generate Premium Commission.

The final result is subject to the system's order status and validity checks.
