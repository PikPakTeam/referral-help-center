# 等级权益什么时候生效？

等级权益会实时生效，系统每日检测等级变化并自动应用相应规则。页面展示可能受数据更新时间影响。

---

## English

### When do level benefits take effect?

Level benefits take effect immediately. The system checks for level changes daily and automatically applies the corresponding rules. What you see on the page may be affected by the data update schedule.
