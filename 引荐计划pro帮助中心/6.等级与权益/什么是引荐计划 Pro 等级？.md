# 什么是引荐计划 Pro 等级？

引荐计划 Pro 等级用于提供相应的存储权益、传输配额权益和超额状态管理规则。

系统会根据计划规则每日检测等级变化，并自动应用对应权益。



---

## English

### What are Referral Program Pro levels?

Referral Program Pro levels provide corresponding storage benefits, Transfer Quota benefits, and over-quota management rules.

The system checks for level changes daily and automatically applies the benefits for your current level according to the program rules.
