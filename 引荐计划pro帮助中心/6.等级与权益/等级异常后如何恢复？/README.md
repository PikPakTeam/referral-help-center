# 等级异常后如何恢复？

您可以继续邀请真实的新用户使用 PikPak。系统后续检测到您的邀请数据恢复正常后，会重新评估并恢复相应的等级权益。

建议避免虚假邀请、重复注册或其他可能被系统判定为异常的行为。

---

## English

### How can I restore my level benefits after a Level Status Alert?

You can continue inviting genuine new users to PikPak. Once the system determines that your referral data has returned to normal, it will reassess and restore the applicable level benefits.

Avoid fake referrals, duplicate registrations, and other activity that may be identified as unusual.
