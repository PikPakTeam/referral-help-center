# 不同等级的存储超额权益有什么区别？

不同等级对应的存储超额处理规则如下：

- \*\*LV0：\*\*存储空间连续超额 4 个月后进入 2 个月宽限期。如果总计 6 个月后仍未处理超额状态，PikPak 将开始清理超出存储配额的文件。
- \*\*LV1：\*\*在 LV0 规则的基础上额外获得 4 个月宽限期。存储空间连续超额 4 个月后，可享有总计 6 个月的宽限期，即最长可在连续超额 10 个月后才开始处理超额文件。
- \*\*LV2 及以上：\*\*维持 LV2 或更高等级期间，存储超额宽限期可持续延长，PikPak 不会清理超出存储配额的文件。

等级发生变化时，存储超额状态的起始时间不会重置，系统会根据累计的超额时间和当前等级重新判断宽限状态。具体状态及剩余时间请以邮件通知为准。

## English

### How do over-quota storage benefits differ by level?

Different levels have different rules for managing storage that exceeds your Storage Quota:

- **LV0:** After your storage remains over quota for four consecutive months, you enter a two-month grace period. If the over-quota status is not resolved after a total of six months, PikPak will begin deleting files that exceed your Storage Quota.
- **LV1:** You receive an additional four-month grace period on top of the LV0 rules. After your storage remains over quota for four consecutive months, you receive a total grace period of six months. This means files exceeding your Storage Quota will not be processed until your storage has remained over quota for up to ten consecutive months.
- **LV2 and above:** While you maintain LV2 or a higher level, the over-quota grace period continues to be extended, and PikPak will not delete files that exceed your Storage Quota.

When your level changes, the start date of your over-quota status is not reset. The system reassesses your grace-period status based on the accumulated over-quota duration and your current level. Please refer to email notifications for your current status and remaining time.
