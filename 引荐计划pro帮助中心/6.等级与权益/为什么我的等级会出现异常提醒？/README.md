# 为什么我的等级会出现异常提醒？

当系统检测到您的邀请数据存在异常时，可能会暂停您的等级权益。例如，您邀请的付费用户数量明显低于引荐计划 Pro 中同等级的其他用户时，页面可能会显示“等级状态异常”。

等级异常仅影响等级对应的存储权益，不影响已经建立的邀请关系和符合规则的收益发放。

---

## English

### Why am I seeing a Level Status Alert?

Your level benefits may be paused if the system detects unusual referral data. For example, a Level Status Alert may appear when the number of paying users you have invited is significantly lower than that of other users at the same Referral Program Pro level.

A Level Status Alert affects only the storage benefits associated with your level. It does not affect existing referral relationships or the payment of eligible earnings.
