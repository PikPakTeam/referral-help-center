# 通过免费方式获得会员会产生会员分成收益吗？

不会。通过免费兑换码、赠送会员时间或其他免费方式获得的会员，不视为成功购买会员，因此不会产生会员分成收益。



---

## English

### Will Premium obtained for free generate Premium Revenue Share?

No. Premium obtained through a free Redeem Code, gifted Premium time, or another free method is not considered a successful Premium purchase and will not generate Premium Revenue Share.
