# 为什么有时候我的收益为负数？

这通常是由于您邀请的用户申请退款导致的。用户成功购买会员后，相关收益会在购买当日计入；如果用户后续退款，对应收益将在退款当日扣除，因此当日收益可能显示为负数。

---

## English

### Why are my earnings sometimes negative?

This usually happens when an invited user requests a refund. Earnings from a successful Premium purchase are recorded on the purchase date. If the user later receives a refund, the corresponding earnings are deducted on the refund date, which may cause your Daily Earnings to appear negative.
