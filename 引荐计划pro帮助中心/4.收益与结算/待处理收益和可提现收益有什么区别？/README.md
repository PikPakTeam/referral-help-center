# 待处理收益和可提现收益有什么区别？

待处理收益是仍处于锁定期内的收益。符合条件的收益在锁定 30 天后，会计入可提现收益。



---

## English

### What's the difference between Pending Earnings and Withdrawable Earnings?

Pending Earnings are earnings that are still within the lock period. Eligible earnings are added to your Withdrawable Earnings after the 30-day lock period ends.
