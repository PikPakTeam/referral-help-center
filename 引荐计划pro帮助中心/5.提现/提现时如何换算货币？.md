# 提现时如何换算货币？

提现金额会根据您选择的收款方式，按处理时适用的汇率进行换算并转账。最终到账金额以实际处理结果为准。

---

## English

### How is currency converted for a withdrawal?

Your withdrawal amount will be converted and transferred using the applicable exchange rate at the time it is processed, based on the Payment Method you select. The final amount received is subject to the actual processing result.
