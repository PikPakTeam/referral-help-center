# 如何修改我的提现方式或提现账号／钱包地址？

您可以进入引荐计划 Pro 的「设置」-「收款方式」页面，修改提现方式、提现账号或钱包地址。  
为保障账户安全，修改时系统可能会要求您通过绑定的邮箱或手机号验证身份。



---

## English

### How do I change my withdrawal method, payment account, or wallet address?

Go to Settings > Payment Method in Referral Program Pro to change your withdrawal method, payment account, or wallet address.

To protect your account, you may be asked to verify your identity using the email address or phone number set on your account.
