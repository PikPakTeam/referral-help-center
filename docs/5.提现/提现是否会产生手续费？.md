# 提现是否会产生手续费？

部分国家或地区的提现可能需要扣除第三方交易手续费或相关税费。实际金额请以提现处理结果和当地适用规定为准。

---

## English

### Are there any withdrawal fees?

Withdrawals in some countries or regions may be subject to third-party transaction fees or applicable taxes. The final amount is subject to the withdrawal result and applicable local requirements.
