# 收益一直不提现会失效吗？

未提现收益自获得之日起两年内有效。超过两年仍未提现的收益可能失效，请及时关注可提现余额和相关记录。

---

## English

### Will my earnings expire if I don't withdraw them?

Unwithdrawn earnings remain valid for two years from the date they are earned. Earnings that are not withdrawn within two years may expire, so please keep an eye on your Withdrawable Earnings and related records.
