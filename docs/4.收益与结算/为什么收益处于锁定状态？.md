# 为什么收益处于锁定状态？

符合条件的收益会有 30 天锁定期。锁定期用于处理退款、异常订单及其他必要核验。

此时收益位于「待处理收益」中。锁定期结束且记录仍符合规则后，收益会计入可提现余额。



---

## English

### Why are my earnings locked?

Eligible earnings are subject to a 30-day lock period to allow time for refunds, abnormal order reviews, and other necessary checks.

During this period, the amount appears under Pending Earnings. Once the lock period ends and the record remains eligible, the amount will be added to your Withdrawable Earnings.
